- Status: WATCH / only realistic live candidate
- Why: 500+ user intranet, estimated value 900,000 DKK excl. VAT, 3-year term with 2x12-month extension options, SME-friendly, below EU threshold but cross-border interest.
- Deadlines:
  - Questions: 2026-08-03 23:59 CEST
  - Request to participate: 2026-08-12 14:00 CEST
  - Offer deadline: 2026-09-09 14:00 CEST
- Risk: procurement docs are behind a byggeprojekt.dk registration flow asking for company/contact details. Minimum participation requirements likely live there.
- Recommendation: only continue if Teddy Apps can quickly field a legal bidding entity and credible references / delivery setup.

### 2. Heidekreis timekeeping solution (Germany, 441830-2026)
- Status: NO
- Why not now: explicit selection criteria require average yearly turnover of at least 460,000 EUR in timekeeping solutions, good credit rating, ISO 9001, ISO/IEC 27001, and at least 3 comparable references.
- Extra complexity: on-prem deployment, data migration, fidelis.Personal interface, training, multi-year support.

### 3. Hannover time recording + personnel planning (Germany, 441817-2026)
- Status: HARD NO
- Why not now: requires at least 10 employees in development/implementation/support, 3 highly specific references, one in clinic/public shift operations, 500,000 EUR average turnover in relevant field, 5 productive DACH installs, DSGVO proof, and SAP/KIDICAP/AD/LDAP/Shibboleth integration capability.

### 4. InGrid further development/hosting (Germany, 441770-2026)
- Status: NO for now
- Why not now: less heavy than the two HR tenders, but still requires 2 comparable references, at least 150,000 EUR yearly turnover in the relevant field, average 3 staff, and prior experience operating metadata systems with GDI-DE-conformant interfaces.
- Also domain-specific and support/hosting-heavy.

## Recommended path from here

1. Do not spend effort on the three German tenders.
2. Keep scanning broadly, but tighten scoring against hard procurement prerequisites:
   - required turnover
   - minimum staff count
   - ISO/security certs
   - number/type of references
   - domain-specific install base
3. Treat Metroselskabet Intranet as the only current candidate worth deeper follow-up.
4. Before bidding anything, assemble a minimal bid-readiness pack:
   - legal bidding entity (CVR or partner entity)
   - one-page company profile
   - 2-3 relevant project references / case studies
   - named delivery lead + support model
   - procurement contact email and tender-platform account
5. If Teddy Apps wants to move from monitoring to bidding, the most realistic route is likely:
   - bid on smaller Danish digital/web/intranet/portal tenders
   - or partner/subcontract under an established vendor for the first public-sector win

## Practical conclusion
The tender radar is useful right now mainly as market intelligence and pipeline-building. It is not yet realistic to treat every interesting tender as bid-ready just because it is technically AI-buildable.

The current best move is to keep monitoring, ignore the German enterprise-heavy ones, and only consider advancing on Danish web/intranet/internal-tool tenders where the qualification bar looks survivable.
